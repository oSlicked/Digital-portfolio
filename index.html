I have been shooting tournament sports since 2017, mostly hockey and lacrosse in arenas across the region, plus race days at the track. On tournament weekends I work with DS Photography, setting up on site and getting photos to families while the event is still going.

Off the clock I slow down with film and handwritten notes in an A5 notebook. Same eye for detail, different pace.
