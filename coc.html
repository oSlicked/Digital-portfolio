    Timeline

    Hatched bars are upgrades running now. Anything that starts left of the orange line gets the Jam price, even if it finishes after. Times are Toronto time and assume you start each upgrade as soon as the builder frees up.

    Builders

    Heroes marked after CWL must stay up until CWL ends; if it runs late, swap them with the Army Camp or Dark Barracks on the same builder. Gold is the limit: if you fall short, drop the final-round Teslas first.

    The Laboratory and Pet House run alongside your builders. Lab times come from your in-game Jam prices minus the Gold Pass 10%; your Lab Assistant can make them shorter. Pets compete with your heroes for Dark Elixir, so only run the Pet House when you have Dark Elixir to spare. Hero equipment uses Ore and isn't discounted by the Jam.

    Magic items

    • Book of Heroes x2: Royal Champion upgrades normally while you sit out CWL days 1–2, so both books are free. Use one on the Grand Warden once you have the Elixir, and keep the other for a hero upgrade after the Jam, when hero upgrades take about 3.5 days.
    • Rune of Dark Elixir: after RC empties your Dark Elixir, use it to fill up for the Archer Queen and King.
    • Book of Everything: save it for after the Jam, on an upgrade that would take 11+ days at full price.
    • Builder Potions: all used on Nov 1, which pulled every builder about a day earlier. That leaves roughly a day of slack before most final starts: if Gold allows, slot in a short upgrade there (Logger, a trap, or a supercharge stage) so no builder sits idle.
    • Research Potions x2: use them while both lab slots are busy and you already have Elixir for the next research; a lab that finishes early with no Elixir gains nothing.
    • Pet Potions x5: only if you are spending Dark Elixir on pets.
    • Wall Rings x4: spend any time; they don't need a free builder.
