Reflection

Me as a digital learner.

When I think about how I actually learn, it has never come from lecture slides or textbook chapters. Reading about something rarely sticks for me. What works is jumping in, making mistakes and figuring out why something broke.

Motive Power made sense to me because I had my hands on the parts instead of just looking at diagrams. Now that I am taking Business General online, I can see that my best work happens when I mix that hands on habit with computers and planning. That is also why I built a website instead of a slideshow. Building it is how I learn it.
